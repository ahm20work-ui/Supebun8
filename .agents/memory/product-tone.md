---
name: Product tone
description: The intended emotional experience for this study-planning app.
---

This app should feel like an emotionally supportive personal space. Productivity should help the user work toward her goals while feeling appreciated, encouraged, and loved; the experience must never feel judgmental.

**Why:** The user described the app as a personal gift and explicitly prioritized care and encouragement alongside functionality.

**How to apply:** Keep future copy, empty states, progress feedback, and interaction design warm and supportive. Avoid language that shames the user for missed tasks or unfinished goals.
